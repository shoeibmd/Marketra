# Paper Trading Risk & Limitations Disclosure

## Disclaimers & Assumptions

1. **Virtual Execution**: All paper trades, account balances, and backtest results are simulated. No live orders are routed to real financial exchanges or brokers.
2. **Slippage & Market Impact**: Backtest simulations model fixed slippage (default 0.05%) and fixed transaction fees (₹20.00). Real market order fills may experience variable liquidity impact, order book depth gaps, or exchange latency not fully captured in paper simulations.
3. **No Forecasts or Guarantees**: Backtesting results reflect historical simulation only. Past performance does not guarantee future financial returns.
